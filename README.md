## > whoami

I've been around technology for as long as I can remember.

As a kid, I was always fascinated by computers, software, games and the idea that technology could be used to actually **build things**.

One of my first structured experiences with computers came through a **Microsoft Office course offered at my school**, where I earned one of my first technology certifications.

But I never stayed on just one side of tech.

My interest eventually took me into games, design and development.

I graduated from **SAGA — School of Art, Game and Animation**, where I studied game development and had contact with the programming behind games as part of that journey.

That was an important point for me.

Technology wasn't only something I enjoyed using anymore.

**I wanted to understand how things were built.**

Later, that journey took me to Australia, where I studied **Information Technology at AIT**.

I didn't complete the program, but studying technology abroad became another important chapter in my path and gave me a different perspective on where I wanted to go professionally.

Back in Brazil, I decided to go deeper into software development.

Today, I'm studying **Software Engineering at FIAP** and working toward becoming a **Full-Stack Developer**.

And the way I like to learn is pretty simple:

> **Build something real. Break it. Figure out why. Make it better.**
