# Hey, I'm Guilherme Trevisan

### Software Engineering @ FIAP 🇧🇷 | IT Studies @ AIT Australia 🇦🇺
### Building my way into Full-Stack Development

I've been around technology for as long as I can remember.

As a kid, I was always fascinated by computers, software, games and the idea that you could actually build things with technology.

One of my first structured experiences with computers came through a Microsoft Office course offered at my school, where I earned my first certification in technology.

But I never really stayed in just one side of tech.

My interest eventually took me into games, design and development.

I graduated from **SAGA — School of Art, Game and Animation**, where I studied game development and had the opportunity to work with the programming behind games as part of that journey.

That was when something I'd always enjoyed started becoming more than just curiosity.

Technology had always been around me — now I was learning how to create with it.

Later, that journey took me all the way to Australia, where I studied **Information Technology at AIT**. I didn't complete the program, but living and studying technology abroad became another important chapter in my path.

Back in Brazil, I decided to take the next step.

Today, I'm studying **Software Engineering at FIAP** and focusing on becoming a **Full-Stack Developer**.

And the way I like to learn is simple:

**Build something real. Break it. Figure out why. Make it better.**

---

## What I'm doing right now

I'm moving beyond exercises and tutorials and putting what I learn into real projects.

One of my current projects is **Hiperion Protocolos**, an internal web application created to digitalize and manage document delivery workflows.

What started as a relatively simple idea has evolved into a system involving:

- Authentication and user access control
- Roles and permissions
- Protocol creation and tracking
- Multiple documents and individual expiration dates
- Digital signatures
- Delivery workflows
- History and audit records
- Offline support and synchronization
- Responsive mobile interfaces
- REST APIs
- SQLite database
- Server deployment and remote access

And that's exactly the kind of development I enjoy.

Not just making a screen look good.

I want to understand what happens behind it.
