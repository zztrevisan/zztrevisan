# Journey

[← Home](../../README.md) · [🇧🇷 Ler em português](../pt-br/jornada.md) · [Next: Learning →](./learning.md)

My path into software has been shaped by curiosity, creative work, international experience and practical projects.

## Early interest

Computers, software and games were my starting point. An early Microsoft Office course was one of my first formal experiences with technology and encouraged me to keep exploring.

## SAGA

At **SAGA — School of Art, Game and Animation**, I studied creative and game-related disciplines and had contact with game development and programming. This period shifted my attention from using digital products to understanding how they are made.

## Australia

I later moved to Australia and studied **Information Technology at AIT — Academy of Information Technology**. I did not complete the program, but the experience strengthened my independence, exposed me to an international learning environment and developed my ability to communicate in English.

I also earned an **Advanced English certification from Australian Pacific College**. Today, I am comfortable using English in technical study and professional communication.

## FIAP and software engineering

After returning to Brazil, I chose to pursue software development more deliberately. I am now studying **Software Engineering at FIAP**, connecting academic foundations with applications built around real operational needs.

## Real-world projects

**Hiperion Protocolos** brought together frontend, backend, a database, authentication, access control, offline behavior, synchronization, testing and infrastructure constraints. **NFSe Hiperion** expanded that learning into business process automation.

These projects changed my view of development: making a feature run is only the beginning. Software also has to fit its users, protect data, communicate failures and operate in a real environment.

```text
Curiosity → SAGA → AIT Australia → FIAP → Real projects → Full-Stack Engineering
```

## Where I am heading

My next step is to begin working professionally with software, contribute with the practical mindset I have developed and deepen my understanding alongside experienced engineers.
