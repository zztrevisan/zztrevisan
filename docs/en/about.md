# About me

[← Home](../../README.md) · [🇧🇷 Ler em português](../pt-br/sobre.md) · [Next: Hiperion Protocolos →](./hiperion-protocolos.md)

I have been interested in technology since my first contact with computers, software and games. That curiosity led me through design and game development at **SAGA — School of Art, Game and Animation**, where I also encountered programming and became increasingly interested in what happens behind the screen.

Later, I lived in Australia and studied **Information Technology at AIT — Academy of Information Technology**. Although I did not complete the program, studying and living abroad became an important part of my development. It gave me international experience and made English a language I can use comfortably in study, work and everyday communication. I also earned an **Advanced English certification from Australian Pacific College**.

Today, I study **Software Engineering at FIAP** and build applications around real needs. These projects have required me to think beyond isolated code: requirements, user workflows, data, authentication, permissions, APIs, offline behavior, synchronization, testing, infrastructure and deployment all affect whether software actually works.

## How I approach development

I prefer to start with the problem rather than the technology:

```text
Real problem → Requirements → Design → Implementation → Testing → Feedback → Improvement
```

My strongest practical experience today is with **JavaScript, Node.js, Express, SQLite, HTML and CSS**. I am expanding that foundation deliberately, without treating every technology I study as something I have already mastered.

I am especially interested in internal systems, backend development, workflow automation, data modeling, offline-capable applications and the path from a local prototype to reliable software in production.

> **Build something real. Break it. Understand why. Make it better.**

## Current goal

I am looking for an opportunity to begin my professional career in software development, contribute to a team and learn from experienced engineers while growing toward Full-Stack Engineering.
